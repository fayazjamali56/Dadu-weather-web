About Dadu Weather

Dadu Weather is a regional weather platform built specifically for Dadu District, Sindh — Dadu town itself, and the towns around it: Johi, Wahi Pandhi, Bhan Syedabad, Mehar, and Khairpur Nathan Shah, plus nearby cities like Moro, Sehwan Sharif, Larkana, and Jamshoro.

Why focus on Dadu District?

Most general weather apps treat small Sindh towns as an afterthought, if they list them at all. This site starts from the opposite direction: Dadu District is the default view, and the rest of the world is out of scope for now.

Supported locations

The current list covers the district's main towns and a handful of neighbouring cities. It's built from a single, expandable location file, so new towns and villages can be added over time without rebuilding the site.

Where the weather data comes from

Current conditions, hourly forecasts, and the 7-day outlook are fetched live for each location's coordinates — nothing is hard-coded. If a request fails, the site says so plainly rather than showing a guessed number.

What's next

Planned additions include favourite locations for registered users, severe weather alerts, temperature and rainfall charts, Sindhi and Urdu language support, and a home-screen app experience.
